How to play

Three rules. Thousands of ways to get stuck.

1. Tap an arrow

Every arrow is a snake of lines with a pointed head. The head shows which way it wants to go.

2. Slide it out

If nothing is in front of its head, the arrow glides off the board along its own path. One down!

3. Don’t crash

Something in the way? The arrow bumps, flashes red and you lose a drop. Clear every arrow to win.

Questions

Is Arrow Escape free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It shows ads (a banner, a full-screen ad every few levels, and optional rewarded ads for extra drops and hints). There are no in-app purchases at the moment.

Do I need internet to play?

No. All 1000 levels are inside the app. Internet is only used to load ads.

What does an ad give me?

Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice. It gives you one extra drop when you run out, or one extra hint when your hints are used up.

How do the levels get harder?

They don’t get easier! Levels 1–100 are 96×96 boards and levels 101–1000 are 128×128, all packed with long chains of blocked arrows. Every level is guaranteed to be solvable.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 and newer, 64-bit phones and tablets.

What data do you collect?

None ourselves – there are no accounts. Google AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID to show ads. See the Privacy Policy for details and how to control it.
